Your balance is everything in your accounts right now. Safe to Spend is what's left once Lysning takes off the money that's already promised before your next payday — or before the end of the month, if you haven't said when you're paid.

## What comes off

- **Money set aside for goals.** It's still in your account, but it's spoken for.
- **Bills due before then** that haven't been paid yet — rent, phone, subscriptions.
- **What's left for everyday spending** — the part of your monthly amounts for groceries, fuel and eating out that you haven't spent yet.
- **Card payments due** before then.

## Where to see it

On Home, the Live balance card names the gap right under your balance — for example *£300 set aside for goals* and *£410 kept for bills and everyday spending until Fri 3 Oct*. Take both away from the balance and you get Safe to Spend.

Tap either line, or *How this is calculated*, to open the breakdown. It starts with the same sum in your own numbers:

> £1,630 in your accounts, minus £300 set aside for goals and £410 kept for bills and everyday spending until Fri 3 Oct, leaves £920 safe to spend.

## Two things it never does

- **Count money before it arrives.** Your pay counts the moment it lands, not the day you expect it.
- **Count a pound twice.** Money set aside for a goal isn't also kept for a bill.

## If it looks too low

Usually a bill you've already paid hasn't been matched to its payment yet, so it's still being kept back. See [how bills work](/help/how-bills-work/).

Card balances aren't in your Live balance at all — they're money owed, not money you have — which is why Home says *Cards are shown separately*.
