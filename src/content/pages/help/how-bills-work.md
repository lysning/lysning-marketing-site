A bill is a fixed amount on a regular schedule — rent, phone, council tax, a subscription. Add one under **Bills** on the Spending screen with its amount, account, how often it comes round and when it starts.

## Before it's due

If it's due before your next payday, Safe to Spend keeps its amount back, so the money's there when it goes out.

## When it's paid

By default Lysning doesn't record the payment for you. When the real payment shows up — entered by hand or imported from a statement — Lysning matches it to the bill: same account, about the same amount, close to the due date. It never matches on the description. If two payments could fit, it waits rather than guessing.

Once matched, the bill leaves the list. Your balance has already dropped by the same amount, so [Safe to Spend doesn't move](/help/why-does-safe-to-spend-change/).

## "Add it for me"

Switch this on for a bill and Lysning records the payment itself on the due date — handy for direct debits you'd never enter by hand. It's off unless you choose it.

## If the payment never turns up

After one billing period — a week for a weekly bill, a month for a monthly one — Lysning stops keeping the bill back, so a missed match doesn't pile up. The bill stays on record, and a payment that arrives later still matches it.
