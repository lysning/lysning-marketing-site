Both shape Safe to Spend, but they do opposite jobs.

## A goal is money you keep

Something you're saving towards — a deposit, a holiday, a cushion — with an amount and a date. Money [set aside](/help/does-set-aside-money-move/) for it stays aside until you spend it on the goal, and it builds up month after month. Goals live on the **Plan** tab.

## Everyday spending is money you mean to spend

A monthly amount for spending that varies — groceries, fuel, eating out. Nothing builds up: it's for this month. Safe to Spend holds back the part you haven't spent yet, so it's still there when the shopping comes round. Going over is shown plainly and never blocked. In the app these are the plans under **Everyday** on the Spending screen.

## And bills

Fixed amounts on known dates — rent, phone, subscriptions. They sit under **Bills** on the same screen. See [how bills work](/help/how-bills-work/).

## Side by side

| | Goal | Everyday spending |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Save towards something | Cover this month's spending |
| Money | Set aside, builds up | Held back until spent |
| Over the line | — | Shown, never blocked |
| In Safe to Spend | What's set aside comes off | What's unspent comes off |
