Safe to Spend covers the stretch until your next payday (or the end of the month). It's worked out again whenever something it depends on changes. Here's what moves it, and what doesn't.

## What moves it

- **Spending.** Spend £20 and it drops by £20.
- **Your pay landing.** Income counts once it's in your account, not before. If a goal is set to *Set aside automatically*, that goal's share is set aside straight away, and Lysning tells you so.
- **A new stretch starting.** After payday the window runs to the next one, and bills due in it start being kept back.
- **Changes you make** — adding a bill, setting more aside for a goal, changing an everyday amount.

## What doesn't

- **Paying a bill it already knew about.** Your balance falls and the bill leaves the list by the same amount, so Safe to Spend stays put. It had already kept that money back.
- **Everyday spending your monthly amount covers.** Groceries within your grocery amount were already kept back, so buying them doesn't change the number.

## The daily figure

Under Safe to Spend, *about £60 a day until 30 Oct* is simply the number shared across the days left. It shifts as the days pass, even when nothing else does.

## A bill that never got matched

If a bill's payment never turns up, Lysning stops keeping it back after one billing period — a week for a weekly bill, a month for a monthly one — so a missed match can't pile up. It stays on record, and a payment that arrives later still matches it. See [how bills work](/help/how-bills-work/).
