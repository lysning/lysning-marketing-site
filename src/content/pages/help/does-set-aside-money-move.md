No. Money set aside for a goal stays in your own bank account. Lysning marks part of your balance as belonging to the goal and stops counting it as spendable. It never holds, moves or invests your money — it isn't connected to your bank, so it couldn't.

## Where you'll see it

- Your **Live balance** still includes it, with *£300 set aside for goals* underneath.
- **Safe to Spend** leaves it out, so the same pound is never promised to a goal and to today's spending at once.

## How money gets set aside

- **When you create a goal**, you can set some aside straight away.
- **On payday**, if the goal is set to *Set aside automatically*. When your income arrives, Lysning sets this month's amount aside and tells you — *Your income arrived. £200 for Holiday is set aside* — with **Adjust** and **Undo**. If there isn't enough for every goal, each gets a share.
- **Whenever you like**, from the goal itself.

You can only set aside money that isn't already promised: if you ask for more, Lysning tells you how much is available.

## Spending it, or changing your mind

- **Spending on the goal** — record it from the goal. That logs the purchase and lowers what's set aside.
- **Releasing money** — it goes straight back into Safe to Spend.
- **Spending it by accident** — your bank won't stop you, and nor will Lysning. It shows what happened and leaves the choice to you: push the goal's date out, or spend a little less until payday.
