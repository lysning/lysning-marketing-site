## What is Safe-to-Spend?

The one number Lysning shows you: what you can spend today without touching money you've already set aside. Not your bank balance — balances lie, what with pending charges, transfers between your own accounts, refunds still on their way back. Safe-to-Spend nets all of that out.

## What's the difference between the payday and month-end horizons, and how does that change the number?

Safe-to-Spend looks ahead, not just at today. It projects to whichever comes next — your payday or month-end — and counts bills due before then against the number now, even ones you haven't paid yet. When the horizon shifts, a new payday hits, the month turns over, the number recalculates to match what's actually still ahead of you.

## How is Safe-to-Spend calculated?

Your real balances, minus whatever's set aside for goals, minus bills due inside the current horizon, with refunds, self-transfers, and money owed back to you netted out of the noise. What's left is what's safe to spend.

## How are goals funded?

You move real money into a goal from your real accounts — nothing leaves your bank, it just gets tagged. Lysning keeps a running ledger of what you've put toward each goal, so it always knows how much of your balance is already spoken for.

## How do set-asides work?

Earmark money for a goal and Lysning subtracts it from what counts as spendable. Available money is your balance minus everything you've set aside — the same pound never gets counted toward a goal and toward today's spending at once.

## What happens if I accidentally spend set-aside money?

It can happen. Lysning never holds your money, so nothing stops you from spending it — the app finds out the same way you do, after the fact. When it does, two options: push the goal out, or cut back on variable spending for the rest of the horizon to close the gap. Lysning won't fix it for you. It'll just keep telling you the truth so you can decide.

## Does Lysning hold or move my money?

No. It stays in your own bank accounts the whole time. Lysning tracks and earmarks — it never takes custody, moves funds, or invests for you.

## Is my data private? Does it ever leave my phone?

On the free tier, everything runs on your device — no account, no server, nothing to breach. The only thing that ever leaves is anonymous usage analytics, opt-in, off by default.

## How accurate is the number, really?

As accurate as what you've entered. The math is straightforward: refunds, self-transfers, and money owed back to you get reconciled out, so what's left is real spendable money — not a balance jumping around for reasons that have nothing to do with what you can actually spend.
