## 3. Your data, and the absence of backup

Your financial data — transactions, balances, accounts, goals, income, categories and settings — is created and stored **only on your device**, encrypted at rest. We hold no copy. This is described in full in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

The direct consequence, which you accept by taking part:

- **We cannot back up, restore, recover, export or delete your data for you.** We have no access to it, so there is nothing at our end to act on.
- **Taking backups is yours to do.** The App gives you the means: **Settings → Back up** writes an encrypted file you keep wherever you choose, and **Settings → Restore** reads one back. **Settings → Export transactions** writes a spreadsheet. Whether a backup exists when you need one is down to you having made it.
- **A backup is only as good as its passphrase.** You choose it, it is never sent anywhere and it is not stored — so if you forget it, that backup cannot be opened by anyone, including us.
- **An exported spreadsheet is not encrypted.** Once you send one somewhere, anyone who can open the file can read your figures. Where it ends up, and who can reach it there, is your choice and your responsibility.
- If you delete the App, reset or lose your device, or a beta build fails or expires, **any data you have not backed up is permanently gone.**
- **Do not use the beta as your only record** of anything that matters. Take backups, and keep your own records — your bank remains the source of truth for your money.
