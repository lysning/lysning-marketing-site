# ADR 0002 — Purpose: explain + build trust; no conversion form

**Status:** locked (user-chosen)

## Context
App not live. User picked "explain + build trust" over waitlist/pre-register as the site's job.
Timeline: weeks (room for design polish).

## Decision
Site's single job is to **explain Orielle and build trust**. No email capture, no waitlist,
no lead form in v1.

## Consequences
- Purely static; no third-party form service needed.
- Success = comprehension + trust, not conversions. No CTA-to-signup.
- Open: is there a soft "notify me at launch" or app-store pre-register link later? (ADR 0006 area.)
