# ADR 0004 — Hero framing: candidate C (hybrid)

**Status:** locked (user-chosen)

## Context
`narrative.md` offers three one-liners; thesis is **results/confidence convert, privacy is
closing proof — never the hook**.

## Decision
Hero uses candidate **C (hybrid)**:

> A goal-first money app that tells you what's truly safe to spend today — and never needs
> your data in the cloud.

Goal-first + Safe-to-Spend leads; the privacy clause sits at the tail as proof, consistent
with the thesis. India-tune the supporting subhead (₹, salaried saver), not the core line.

## Consequences
- Hero visual = a calm Safe-to-Spend screen (real screenshot, ADR 0007).
- No signup CTA (ADR 0002); hero CTA is a scroll/learn-more or app-store "coming soon" placeholder.
- Keep A and B as stored copy variants for future store-listing / A-B tests.
